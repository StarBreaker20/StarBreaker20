## Selected work

### [rendrr — deterministic video editing and rendering](https://github.com/StarBreaker20/Renderer)

[![rendrr video catalog](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/StarBreaker20/Renderer/main/docs/rendrr-catalog.jpg)](https://github.com/StarBreaker20/Renderer)

A Next.js and Remotion video editor where the browser preview, paused-frame
filmstrip, and Lambda export share one composition. Overlays are stored in
composition coordinates so preview and final render stay frame-accurate.

`TypeScript` · `Next.js` · `Remotion` · `Zustand` · `AWS Lambda`

### [Agentic Premiere — AI editing inside Premiere Pro](https://github.com/StarBreaker20/agentic-premiere-case-study)

[![Agentic Premiere sequence analysis](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/StarBreaker20/agentic-premiere-case-study/main/assets/smart-analysis.png)](https://github.com/StarBreaker20/agentic-premiere-case-study)

A human-in-the-loop editing architecture for Adobe Premiere Pro: sandboxed UXP
execution, typed AI plans, clone-first timeline changes, Gemini Vision analysis,
and media services behind a controlled approval boundary.

`Software Architecture` · `Adobe UXP` · `AI Agents` · `FastAPI` · `FFmpeg`

### [SG-GNN — spike-gated graph neural network research](https://github.com/StarBreaker20/sg-gnn-research-case-study)

A ten-stage research program combining message-passing graph networks with
leaky integrate-and-fire neurons for binary edge-existence decisions. The
code-free case study documents the QM9 and ZINC experiments, ablations,
negative results, and limitations while the research implementation stays private.

`Python` · `PyTorch` · `PyTorch Geometric` · `SpikingJelly` · `GNNs`
